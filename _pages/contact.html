01 PhD

PhD Students

We are looking for PhD students with a solid research foundation in computer security or closely related areas, who are interested in tackling real-world security problems through rigorous research and hands-on system building.

Basic Requirements
  • A research background in computer security or related areas, including software engineering, operating systems, human-computer interaction, artificial intelligence, and related fields.
  • Prior research and publication experience is required. Applicants should have at least one representative first-author paper.

02 MASTER

Master's Students

We welcome motivated Master's students who want to gain rigorous research experience and grow from learning cybersecurity to actually doing security research.

We value students who
  • Are genuinely interested in cybersecurity and real-world systems.
  • Enjoy coding, experimentation, and learning by building.
  • Are willing to read papers critically and ask their own research questions.
  • Want to challenge themselves with meaningful research problems.
Hands-on experience is especially welcome.

Students with experience in CTF, vulnerability discovery, reverse engineering, software systems, or substantial coding projects are particularly encouraged to apply.

HOW TO APPLY

Ready to join AIMS?

If you are interested in pursuing a PhD at AIMS, please send a concise introduction together with the required materials listed here.

Please note: inquiries from PhD applicants without the required materials listed here may not receive a response.

Required materials for PhD applicants
01
Curriculum Vitae (CV)

Please include your education, research experience, publications, projects, awards, technical skills, and other relevant experience.

02
Representative First-Author Paper

Please attach at least one representative research paper on which you are the first author.

FIRST EMAIL

Keep it simple.

A short and informative email is enough. Tell us who you are, what you have worked on, and why AIMS is a good match for the research you would like to pursue.

Subject PhD Application – Your Name – Your University
Attach CV + First-Author Representative Paper
Briefly introduce your current program and research background. Highlight your strongest research or engineering experience, and tell us what security problems you are interested in exploring.

If applicable, feel free to mention your experience in reverse engineering, vulnerability research, CTF, system building, open-source projects, or prior publications.
